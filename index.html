Simple by design

Keep your records close. Back them up when you choose.

Selfcash is built around an on-device financial database. Connecting Google Drive is a separate step that lets the app create or update its own backup file.

  • Enter and manage your financial records in the Android app.
  • Connect the Google account you want to use for your backup.
  • Restore the backup on a device using the same Google account.
01
Sign in with Google

Google provides the account profile details used for the Selfcash session.

02
Keep a local record

Accounts, categories, transactions, people and pocket records are stored in the app's local Room database.

03
Authorise Drive App Data

Grant the app-specific Drive permission to let Selfcash save or restore its backup file.

04
Check backup status

Selfcash displays backup status and the last completed backup time in the app.

Clear about your data

Google Drive access, kept to the app's backup purpose.

Selfcash requests a dedicated app-data permission for backup and restore. It is not designed to browse your ordinary Drive files.

Drive permission used

App-specific storage

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This permission is used to create, find, update and download Selfcash's own backup in the hidden appDataFolder area of the connected Google Drive account.

What goes into a backup?

The backup contains the financial records needed to restore your Selfcash workspace: account and category records, ledger entries, people/receivable/payable records, pocket entries and backup metadata.

Read the full Privacy Policy →

Important backup security note. The current app source serialises the backup as JSON and sends it to Google Drive over HTTPS. The reviewed code does not encrypt the backup JSON with a separate Selfcash-controlled encryption layer before upload. Do not treat the current Drive backup as end-to-end encrypted.
