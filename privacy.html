Selfcash Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Selfcash Android app uses, where your financial records are stored, how Google Sign-In and Google Drive App Data are used, and what choices are available in the current app version.

1. Who we are

Selfcash is a personal finance record-keeping application for Android. It helps you record income, expenses, account balances, transfers, receivables, payables and pocket-money activity. Selfcash is not a bank, payment service, lender, credit provider or financial adviser.

This policy describes the current app implementation reviewed for the Selfcash website. If the app's data practices change, this policy must be updated before those changes are released.

3. Where your information is stored

On your Android device

The app's financial records are stored in a local Room database in Selfcash's private app storage. App preferences and sign-in session details are stored locally using Android DataStore. The app does not need a separate Selfcash-operated financial database to keep these records.

Monthly reports can be generated as PDF files and saved to a location you choose through Android's document/save interface. A PDF that you save or share is then handled by the destination you select.

Android system backup

The current Android manifest enables Android's app backup capability. The supplied app source does not define explicit include/exclude entries in its Android backup-rule files. Android operating-system backup or device-transfer behaviour may therefore apply according to the Android version, device configuration and Google/device backup settings. This is separate from Selfcash's own Google Drive App Data backup.

4. Google Sign-In

Selfcash uses Google's Android sign-in/credential flow. When you choose a Google account, Google provides account information such as your email address and display name; a profile photo URL may also be available. The app uses these details to display your account profile and identify the Selfcash session.

The current app uses the Google account email as its backup-matching identifier. The sign-in ID token is used during the sign-in flow; the reviewed code extracts the profile photo URL from it and does not use that token as a financial record or include it in the Selfcash backup payload.

Google processes sign-in information under Google's own privacy terms. See Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google Drive App Data backup

Google Drive backup is an optional, separately authorised feature. It runs only after you connect Google Drive and grant Selfcash the requested Drive App Data permission.

Permission requested

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This is the Google Drive app-data scope. Selfcash uses it to access its app-specific appDataFolder area. The reviewed code searches for a file named selfcash_backup.json, creates it when needed, updates the existing Selfcash backup, and downloads it for restore. It does not use this scope to list or manage your ordinary Drive documents, photos or other user files.

What the backup contains

  • Account records, including account names, types, balances and optional account numbers.
  • Category records and their category/parent information.
  • Ledger entries, including transaction type, amount, date, description and account/category/person references.
  • People records for receivables and payables, including names, optional phone numbers, amounts and notes.
  • Pocket entries, including top-ups and expenses, with amounts, dates and descriptions.
  • Backup metadata such as the Google-account matching identifier, app/schema version and export timestamp.

Automatic backup and restore

After Drive is connected, Selfcash can schedule a background backup when local financial data changes. Android WorkManager runs the task when its network requirement and Android scheduling conditions are met. A backup may be delayed or fail if the device is offline, Google authorisation needs attention, or Android pauses background work. Check the backup status shown in the app before relying on a backup.

Restore downloads the Selfcash backup from the same Google account and replaces the financial records on the device after validation. The app includes checks intended to avoid restoring a backup associated with a different Google account.

Important: current backup files are JSON, not separately encrypted by Selfcash.

The reviewed backup code serialises the backup payload as JSON and uploads/downloads it through Google's HTTPS Drive API. It does not apply a separate Selfcash-controlled encryption layer to the JSON before upload. Do not describe the current backup as end-to-end encrypted. Google Drive's own security and account protections are governed by Google.

Google processes data stored in Drive under its own terms and privacy policy. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Drive app data documentation.

6. Sharing and third-party services

Selfcash does not sell your personal or financial records. In the source supplied for this review, no Selfcash-operated remote financial database, advertising SDK or analytics SDK was identified. Financial records are kept in the device's local database and, if you authorise it, the app sends a backup payload directly to Google Drive using Google's API.

Information is processed by Google when you use Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup. Android and your device manufacturer may also process app data through operating-system features such as device backup, biometric authentication and file saving. Their handling is subject to their own terms and settings.

If you choose to save or share a generated PDF using another app or service, that destination receives the file according to your action and the destination provider's practices.

7. Security

Selfcash uses Android's private app storage for its local database and preferences. The app also offers optional balance hiding, a five-digit App Lock PIN and biometric unlocking on supported devices. The current PIN implementation stores a salted password-derived verifier for new installations; biometric checks are handled by Android.

Drive API communication in the reviewed code uses HTTPS. However, the current backup JSON is not separately encrypted by Selfcash before it is stored in Drive. Protect your Android device and Google account, use a device screen lock, and do not share your account credentials.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your app and Android system up to date and check that backups have completed before changing devices or removing local app data.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local records

Local financial records remain in the app's private database until they are removed by an available app/device operation or the app's data is cleared/uninstalled. Clearing app data or uninstalling Selfcash can remove local records. Keep a current backup before doing so.

Google Drive backup

The Selfcash backup file remains in the connected Google account's app-specific Drive data area until it is replaced or deleted through a supported operation. The current app source reviewed for this policy does not expose a working in-app control to delete the cloud backup or to delete the Selfcash account.

Current-version limitation

The Settings screen in the supplied app source labels “Delete Account”, “Delete All Data” and “Export All Data” as “Coming soon” and states that these actions are not active yet. Do not rely on those controls as available deletion features in the current version. Contact the developer for help or clarification before entering information that you may later need to remove.

Signing out should not be treated as deleting your financial database or an existing Drive backup. Revoking Selfcash's Google access prevents future access after revocation, but it does not necessarily delete a backup that is already stored in your Google account.

9. Your choices and controls

  • Google sign-in: choose the Google account you want to use for your Selfcash session.
  • Drive backup permission: grant the Drive App Data scope when connecting backup. Without the required authorisation, Selfcash cannot read or write its Drive App Data backup.
  • Google access: review or revoke Selfcash's Google account connection at Google Account connections. Revoking access may stop backup/restore until you connect again; it does not itself guarantee deletion of an existing Drive backup.
  • App privacy settings: use Hide Balances, App Lock and biometric options where available on your device.
  • Monthly PDF: choose where to save a generated report, or cancel the save action.
  • Questions or deletion requests: contact the developer using the details below. The current app version does not yet provide a self-service cloud-backup deletion control.

10. Children's privacy

Selfcash is a general personal finance record-keeping tool and is not designed as a service for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.
